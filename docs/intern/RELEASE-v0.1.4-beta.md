# PES M v0.1.4-beta

The season around the matches: an award season voted by the real rules, a real European framework, transfers that feel like events, and a coach with a life of his own.

## New

**Awards**
- World › Awards, in gala colours:
  - The **Ballon d'Or** night covers the season before. The nominees come out on 8 September and the awards are handed out on 26 October: Ballon d'Or, Kopa, Yashin, Gerd Müller, Johan Cruyff and team of the year. One journalist from each of the top 100 nations ranks his top ten (15 … 1 points).
  - **The Best FIFA awards** cover the calendar year. National coaches, captains, journalists and fans vote 5/3/1. There are awards for player, keeper and coach, plus the FIFA 11 and a fair play award.
- Your first season already has an award night, built from estimated starting values. The previews show bookmakers' odds, and the results show the points and how some voters decided.
- League awards for every country under their real names, voted at the end of the season: Fußballer des Jahres, Torjägerkanone, the Fritz Walter medals, PFA, Pichichi, Zamora and more.
- Players from leagues without detailed stats are estimated, so the awards cover the whole world.

**Europe**
- UEFA five-year ranking for countries and clubs, plus a world club ranking.
- Squad registration for the Champions League, Europa League and Conference League:
  - List A holds 25 players. List B is free for your own players up to 21, from the first team, second team or academy.
  - Unregistered players can't play.
  - You get three changes in the winter window.
- Nations League with Leagues A–D, groups of four and six matchdays in the autumn windows.
- Rankings (scorers, assists, grades, clean sheets) for every competition, including the CL, EL, UECL and cups.
- National cups and leagues as in reality: rounds, entry tiers, amateur clubs, EFL Cup, super cups and the Conference League format.

**Transfers**
- Every move in your negotiations reaches the inbox, with a button that takes you back to the talks.
- Transfer reporters ask whether you're negotiating, depending on the size of the deal. Your answer has consequences.
- Fans, press and your captain have their say on big signings and renewals.
- Your second team signs free agents itself when its squad runs thin. Partner clubs always ask about your loan-list players.
- Transfer windows per country (South America, USA, Asia, Saudi Arabia).

**Players and squad**
- Ratings move with what happens, and you see the reason: a brace, a hat-trick, three goals conceded, morale on the floor, fatigue.
- Retraining to a new position takes weeks, and your assistant suggests sensible switches.
- The squad page opens as a list, with each player's last five grades as bars.
- The dressing room shows countrymen, close friends, tensions, and what is on the players' minds.

**Coach's private life**
- Stress with reasons. Above 70, your team talks work weaker.
- Home (rent or buy, move or commute), monthly bank statement with tax by country, lifestyle levels.
- Partner, anniversaries, children, family moments, and a private calendar.
- Car and motorbike for home visits to players, flight class, staff, personal sponsors, language courses.

**Look**
- A visual match report: score band in club colours, two-sided stat bars, grades by unit, a goal timeline.
- Every inbox message about a player opens with his banner: photo, rating, morale, fitness.
- "Per competition" theme: on matchdays the UI wears the competition's colours. The hub shows the next match's competition with its logo.
- Option: a slim world backup for new careers (database only). It's off by default.

## Fixes
- Deadline day: the summer window now ends on 31 August.
- Club colours came out random in game-database worlds (Bayern was green). They now come from the crest.
- The live ticker bars always use the team colour.
- "Find one like him" no longer hangs.
- White competition logos are visible on light pages.
- Messages name who is speaking and which teammate a problem is about.
- Old saves whose academy was all rated 66 are re-rolled once.
- A sold player could be passed on by the AI in the same window.
