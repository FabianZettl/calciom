# PES M v0.1.4-beta

The season around the matches: an award season voted by the real rules, a real European framework, transfers that feel like events, and a coach with a life of his own.

## New

**Awards**
- World › Awards, in gala colours:
  - The **Ballon d'Or** night covers the season before. The nominees come out on 8 September and the awards are handed out on 26 October: Ballon d'Or, Kopa, Yashin, Gerd Müller, Johan Cruyff and team of the year. One journalist from each of the top 100 nations ranks his top ten (15 … 1 points).
  - **The Best FIFA awards** cover the calendar year. National coaches, captains, journalists and fans vote 5/3/1. There are awards for player, keeper and coach, plus the FIFA 11 and a fair play award.
- Your first season already has an award night, built from estimated starting values. The previews show bookmakers' odds, and the results show the points and how some voters decided.
- League awards for every country under their real names, voted at the end of the season: Fußballer des Jahres, Torjägerkanone, the Fritz Walter medals, PFA, Pichichi, Zamora and more.
- Players from leagues without detailed stats are estimated, so the awards cover the whole world.

**Europe**
- UEFA five-year ranking for countries and clubs, plus a world club ranking.
- Squad registration for the Champions League, Europa League and Conference League:
  - List A holds 25 players. List B is free for your own players up to 21, from the first team, second team or academy.
  - Unregistered players can't play.
  - You get three changes in the winter window.
- Nations League with Leagues A–D, groups of four and six matchdays in the autumn windows.
- Rankings (scorers, assists, grades, clean sheets) for every competition, including the CL, EL, UECL and cups.
- National cups and leagues as in reality: rounds, entry tiers, amateur clubs, EFL Cup, super cups and the Conference League format.

**Competitions and stats**
- A fixtures page for every competition: all rounds with dates and results, opening at the next round.
- Tables show goal difference. Last season's final table and top scorers stay viewable.
- New page Competitions › Club stats: goals and xG for and against, xG difference, shots, shots on target, big chances, possession, pass accuracy and tackles for every club, per competition, this season and last. Click a column to sort.
- Super cups use the champion of the season that just ended. England's league cup is the Carabao Cup.
- Kick-off times per league, like the real ones (Friday night, Saturday afternoon, Sunday evening …). Clubs with a cup or European match close by play on a day that fits.
- The calendar shows each competition in its colour with the kick-off time, plus award galas, international breaks and the big tournaments (AFCON, Asian Cup, EURO, World Cup). You can plan a talk with a player on a day, and it reaches your inbox on that day.
- Champions League draw: no opponent from your own country in the league phase, and at most two from the same other country.

**Transfers**
- Every move in your negotiations reaches the inbox, with a button that takes you back to the talks.
- Transfer reporters ask whether you're negotiating, depending on the size of the deal. Your answer has consequences.
- Fans, press and your captain have their say on big signings and renewals.
- Your second team signs free agents itself when its squad runs thin. Partner clubs always ask about your loan-list players.
- Transfer windows per country (South America, USA, Asia, Saudi Arabia).

- Contract talks with your own players are possible at any time, not only in the last 18 months.
- The summer window opens on 1 July.
- Players on another club's loan list can be requested on loan straight from the transfer market.
- Scouting assignments have a reach: league, country, continent or worldwide.

**Players and squad**
- Ratings move with what happens, and you see the reason: a brace, a hat-trick, three goals conceded, morale on the floor, fatigue.
- Retraining to a new position takes weeks, and your assistant suggests sensible switches.
- The squad page opens as a list, with each player's last five grades as bars.
- The dressing room shows countrymen, close friends, tensions, and what is on the players' minds.

- Change a shirt number in the player profile banner while a window is open. FL27 gets the new number.
- No more playing-time complaints in the off-season.
- Dynamic potential: a young player's season moves his ceiling, up for a strong season and down for a weak one.
- Form held for three weeks becomes the new standard: up to three rating points a season, never past the potential.
- Squad registration rebuilt: list A, list B and not registered as three cards, with list B explained. The '+' button registers second-team and academy players with a free shirt number. The inbox reminds you a week before the first European match and when the winter window opens.

**Skill Tree**
- Players earn Skill Points from grades, man of the match, cup and European nights, wins against stronger sides and milestones. They spend them in a ball of six role trees and a Positions branch. You spend them yourself, or your assistant suggests a node for everyone once a week and buys it.
- You earn Coaching Points from the season goal, titles, promotion and a new licence, and spend them in five branches: Tactics, People, Development, Market and Media.

**FL27**
- "Confirm result" books the score as it stands, for the times PES M doesn't catch the final whistle.
- Friendlies you play in FL27 count like real matches.
- Your set-piece takers and captain go to FL27's game plan. They show up after FL27's next start.
- The formations with and without the ball can be edited freely, player by player, and go to FL27.
- Scoreboard, walk-out and referee follow the competition: the FA Cup looks like the FA Cup. PESM-LivePatch 1.1 makes this work without a patched CommonLib.
- New players and academy graduates get a face in FL27 straight away.

**Club and people**
- A sponsor pyramid: main sponsor, kit supplier, stadium name, two secondary sponsors and four perimeter boards. Sponsors ask several times a season through the inbox, and you can accept, ask for more or decide later.
- Now and then an investor wants to buy in, from a Gulf state fund to a local entrepreneur (only a minority stake in Germany, 50+1). That means money, higher expectations and fans with an opinion.
- More milestones for you (derby win, European nights, 100 wins, three trophies …) and new ones for your players: debut, first goal, hat-trick, 100 games, 50 goals, first cap. The big ones get a full-screen moment.
- Players come to talks in their own tone: furious, demanding, hurt, unsure or polite, depending on mood and character. They react to your answer in their own way too.
- Real reporters write for their actual outlets. Where that couldn't be confirmed, the reporter is invented.

**Coach's private life**
- Stress with reasons. Above 70, your team talks work weaker.
- Home (rent or buy, move or commute), monthly bank statement with tax by country, lifestyle levels.
- Partner, anniversaries, children, family moments, and a private calendar.
- Car and motorbike for home visits to players, flight class, staff, personal sponsors, language courses.

**Look**
- The newspaper page looks like a real paper: masthead, lead story, three columns.
- Sponsor and investor offers arrive as offer cards with the figures at a glance.
- Four new player talks: penalties, rest, the armband, a sponsor appearance.
- A visual match report: score band in club colours, two-sided stat bars, grades by unit, a goal timeline.
- Every inbox message about a player opens with his banner: photo, rating, morale, fitness.
- "Per competition" theme: on matchdays the UI wears the competition's colours. The hub shows the next match's competition with its logo.
- Option: a slim world backup for new careers (database only). It's off by default.
- Full-screen moments rebuilt: titles with the trophy, crests and confetti, awards with a golden ball, derby and final days with floodlights and the competition's colour, signings and new contracts with the shirt.
- Settings › Features: switch private life, sponsor and investor events, media extras, milestone popups, the Skill Tree, player talks and manager extras on or off, also in a running save.

## Fixes
- pesm.log is written on every start (Windows: %APPDATA%\pesm\pesm.log), so it's always there for bug reports.
- The Community Shield could take the wrong champion.
- A crash in the loan dialog.
- Settings › Display: the rows no longer overlap.
- Deadline day: the summer window now ends on 31 August.
- Club colours came out random in game-database worlds (Bayern was green). They now come from the crest.
- The live ticker bars always use the team colour.
- "Find one like him" no longer hangs.
- White competition logos are visible on light pages.
- Messages name who is speaking and which teammate a problem is about.
- Old saves whose academy was all rated 66 are re-rolled once.
- A sold player could be passed on by the AI in the same window.
- Choosing the SiderAddons folder without an extracted world ended with "no Competition.bin". PES M now says so and reads the database straight from the FL27 .cpk files. The option on the first career step is labelled clearly.
- Careers read from the .cpk files no longer fail on a missing character/0 folder.
- A retrained player's new main position goes to FL27, so FL27 no longer rates him at the old one.
- Characters the fonts lack (arrows, ticks, emoji) no longer show as boxes.
