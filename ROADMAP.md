# Roadmap

What is planned for CalcioM, not yet built. Finished changes are in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Before the match in CalcioM (built, in the next release)
- **Match length as a dropdown.** Today it is a row of buttons on the matchday page, with direct start only.
- **Difficulty as a dropdown** for the match in FL27.
- **Weather as a dropdown,** with the option **Realistic**: CalcioM picks the weather that fits the city, the kick-off time and the month.
- **Kit choice in CalcioM.** The kits from the kitserver (Sider) can be picked before the match, for both teams; CalcioM sets the choice before kick-off. The kick-off screen shows both kits as a 2D picture, the hub's next-match card a small one.

## Matches of every club (built, in the next release)
- **A page for every club** with its matches so far and the details of each match.
  - **Left:** the season's matches by month: date, time, opponent with crest, home/away/neutral, result with a win/draw/loss dot (penalty shoot-outs marked), and the competition with round and leg. The season can be chosen.
  - **Right:** the chosen match: date, competition, stadium, crowd, score with crests, both teams' scorers with minute, and the players with ratings, subs and cards.
  - **Below:** previous meetings and a short match report.

## Mod store
CalcioM itself ships no licensed data, which keeps the door open for a Steam or itch.io release. Players add mod sources (repositories) themselves, and CalcioM downloads data packs and mods from them. Players can also build their own packs, submit them and ask for new ones.
- **Pack types:** real newspapers and reporters (off by default), ready-made player faces, talk scenarios, real club, stadium and cup names, cup and league rules, languages, coach lists, rumour texts, music, UI themes.
- **Sources and licences:** you choose the sources, and CalcioM warns before adding one that is not official. Every pack shows its licence.
- **Data only:** no game files and nothing executable. Every pack is checked before it goes in, and each can be switched on and off.
- **Submitting:** via GitHub or Discord, with a wish list for packs people want.
